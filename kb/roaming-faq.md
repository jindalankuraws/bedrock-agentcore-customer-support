# Roaming FAQ

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and include GST.
This document is fictional and is used for a demonstration only.

## Is roaming switched on by default?

No. Data roaming is **off by default** on all plans. You can switch it on in the
app or by asking our customer service team. Switching roaming on is free; you pay
only for what you use.

## How much does roaming data cost?

Roaming data is **not** included in your monthly plan allowance and is charged
separately.

| Destination | Pay-per-use rate | Daily pass |
|---|---|---|
| Malaysia | SGD 5.00 per GB | SGD 5.00 per day, 2 GB included |
| Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines | SGD 8.00 per GB | SGD 8.00 per day, 2 GB included |
| Rest of Asia (including Japan, Korea, China) | SGD 12.00 per GB | SGD 12.00 per day, 3 GB included |
| Europe, United Kingdom | SGD 15.00 per GB | SGD 15.00 per day, 3 GB included |
| Rest of world | SGD 20.00 per GB | SGD 20.00 per day, 3 GB included |

A daily pass runs for 24 hours from first use. Once the data in a daily pass is
used up, further data on that day is charged at the pay-per-use rate.

## How are roaming charges shown on my bill?

Roaming appears as a separate line item naming the country, for example
"Roaming data - Malaysia (1GB)". It is never counted against your plan data.

## Can I set a roaming spend cap?

Yes. The default roaming spend cap is **SGD 100 per billing month**. When you
reach the cap, roaming data stops until you raise the cap or the next billing
month begins. You can ask customer service to change or remove the cap.

## Do calls and SMS while overseas use my plan allowance?

No. The talk time and SMS included in your plan apply to Singapore only.
Calls and SMS made while overseas are charged at the roaming rates above.

## I was charged for roaming but I did not travel

Raise this with our customer service team. A human reviews the case and, if the
charge is wrong, it is removed from your bill. See *Fees and Charges* for how
waivers are handled.
