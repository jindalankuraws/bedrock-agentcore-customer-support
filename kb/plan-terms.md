# Plan Terms and Conditions

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and include GST.
This document is fictional and is used for a demonstration only.

## Plans on offer

| Plan | Monthly fee | Data | Talk time | SMS | Minimum contract |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value 50 | 9.90 | 50 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 | None |
| Lite 150 | 14.95 | 150 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 | None |
| Max 1TB | 17.95 | 1000 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 | None |

All three plans are **SIM-only and month to month**. There is no minimum contract
period unless you took a handset bundle, in which case the contract end date is
shown on your account.

## Billing month

The billing month runs from the 1st to the last day of the calendar month.
The monthly fee is charged in advance. Your bill is issued after the month ends
and is due 21 days later.

## Changing your plan

- You can change plan at any time. There is **no plan change fee**.
- A plan change takes effect on the **1st day of the next billing month**.
  Your current plan and its data allowance continue until then.
- You keep your phone number when you change plan.
- There is no limit on how often you change plan.
- A plan change is confirmed with you before it is made. Nothing changes on your
  account until you confirm.

## Data allowance

- Data is counted per billing month and does **not** roll over to the next month.
- Unused data is lost at the end of the billing month.
- Once your allowance is used up, excess data is charged per GB.
  See *Fees and Charges*.
- Data used while overseas is charged as roaming and does not come out of your
  plan allowance. See *Roaming FAQ*.

## Talk time and SMS

Talk time and SMS apply to local Singapore numbers only. International calls and
calls made while overseas are charged separately.

## Fair use

These plans are for personal use. We may contact you if usage suggests commercial
resale or use as a fixed broadband replacement.

## Handset bundles

If you took a handset with your plan, a minimum contract applies and the contract
end date is shown on your account. Ending a handset bundle early incurs an early
termination charge. See *Contract Termination*.
