# Fees and Charges

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and include GST.
This document is fictional and is used for a demonstration only.

## Monthly plan fees

| Plan | Monthly fee | Data | Talk time | SMS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value 50 | 9.90 | 50 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 |
| Lite 150 | 14.95 | 150 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 |
| Max 1TB | 17.95 | 1000 GB | 1000 minutes | 1000 |

The monthly fee is charged in advance on the first day of each billing month.

## Excess data

If you use more than the data included in your plan, excess data is charged at
**SGD 3.00 per GB**, rounded up to the next whole GB.

Example: a Value 50 customer who uses 52 GB in a month is charged
SGD 9.90 for the plan plus SGD 6.00 for 2 GB of excess data, giving a total of SGD 15.90.

You can avoid excess data charges by moving to a larger plan before the end of the
billing month. See *Plan Terms and Conditions*.

## Late payment fee

A **late payment fee of SGD 10.00** is added to your next bill if payment is not
received by the due date shown on the bill. The due date is normally 21 days after
the end of the billing month.

Only one late payment fee is charged per bill, however often the bill is overdue.

## Roaming charges

Roaming data outside Singapore is charged separately and is not included in your
plan allowance. See *Roaming FAQ* for rates and daily passes.

## Plan change fee

There is **no fee to change your plan**. See *Plan Terms and Conditions* for when
the change takes effect.

## Waivers

You can ask our customer service team to waive a fee. Waivers are not automatic:
the agent raises a request and a human reviews it. Waivers are normally considered
for a first late payment, or where there is a billing error.
