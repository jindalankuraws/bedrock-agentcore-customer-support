# Contract Termination

All prices are in Singapore dollars (SGD) and include GST.
This document is fictional and is used for a demonstration only.

## Cancelling a SIM-only plan

Value 50, Lite 150 and Max 1TB are month-to-month SIM-only plans with **no minimum
contract**. You can cancel at any time with **30 days' notice**. There is no early
termination charge.

Your service continues until the end of the 30-day notice period, and you are
billed as normal for that period. Any unused data is lost and is not refunded.

## Cancelling a handset bundle before the contract ends

If you took a handset with your plan, a minimum contract applies and the contract
end date is shown on your account. Ending it early incurs an **early termination
charge**.

The early termination charge is:

> the remaining months on the contract x the monthly fee of your plan

Example: a Lite 150 customer with 4 months left pays 4 x SGD 14.95 = SGD 59.80.

The charge appears on your final bill. Notice of 30 days is still required.

## Keeping your number

You can keep your mobile number when you move to another provider. Ask the new
provider to start the port-in; do **not** cancel with us first, because cancelling
releases the number.

## Final bill

Your final bill is issued after the service ends and covers:

- the monthly fee up to the end of the notice period,
- any excess data or roaming used in that period,
- any early termination charge,
- any unpaid balance from earlier bills.

The final bill is due 21 days after it is issued. The late payment fee in
*Fees and Charges* applies to the final bill in the same way.

## Cancelling because of a fault or a dispute

If you are cancelling because of a service fault or a billing dispute, tell our
customer service team before you give notice. A human reviews the case, and the
early termination charge may be waived. Waivers are never automatic.

## Death or permanent relocation

Contracts can be ended without an early termination charge where the account
holder has died or is permanently leaving Singapore. Supporting documents are
required. These cases are always handled by a human, never automatically.
